# Toxoplasma design references

## Verified publication

Bergersen KV, Barnes A, Worth D, David C, Wilson EH. **Targeted Transcriptomic Analysis of C57BL/6 and BALB/c Mice During Progressive Chronic Toxoplasma gondii Infection Reveals Changes in Host and Parasite Gene Expression Relating to Neuropathology and Resolution.** *Frontiers in Cellular and Infection Microbiology*. 2021;11:645778. Published 18 March 2021. [DOI: 10.3389/fcimb.2021.645778](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcimb.2021.645778).

The original figure-design review verified metadata against the PDF title page and inspected Figures 1-3 with their captions on pages 5-7. The [source record](docs/figure-redesign/literature-evidence.json) retains the DOI, article checksum and inspection scope. The article PDF and inspection renders are not distributed with this repository. Phase 2 preserves this historical record; it does not claim a new full-paper visual review or additional design influences.

## Principles transferred

Figure 1 organizes expression structure, sample-level PCA, time-specific differential expression and overlap into distinct labeled panels. Its repeated time-point panels motivated the aligned 11/33-dpi volcanoes and consistent axis definitions here. Figure 2 keeps time-point identities coherent across a focused expression heatmap and individual-gene panels. This supports explicit study-group blocks in the supplementary heatmaps. Figure 3 connects pathway-scale patterns with individual-gene views and displays reference levels in captions; this motivated a small, explicitly selected immune-theme comparison linked to the full GO rankings.

These are compositional and labeling principles, not copied geometry, artwork or numerical methods. The reference uses targeted NanoString panels and 14/28/56-dpi comparisons in different strain contexts; this project retains its own bulk RNA-seq libraries at 11/33 dpi, original statistical definitions and whole-brain interpretation. The reference's multiple adjusted-P lines are not imported into this project's fixed primary selection.

## Distinct visual identity

The project uses an amber/rust infection-stage palette, open/filled sample symbols, blue for downward effects, restrained slate text and clear quantitative legends. PCA and agreement axes preserve equal units. Comparative panels carry counts and conditional qualifiers beside the evidence. The aesthetic emphasizes comparative transcriptomics and systems immunology, without journal logos or publisher branding. It is intentionally distinct from the M1 Research Internship methods palette and AD neurogenomics palette.
