# Supplementary computational information

This supplement links the files and execution records supporting the RNA-seq reanalysis. Scientific methods remain in the report and the [statistical specification](../docs/statistical-analysis.md).

## Execution and available artifacts

The 12-library analysis of PRJNA483261 completed on 28 October 2026 UTC. Execution records, model diagnostics and numerical checks are retained in the [historical validation record](../docs/validation-status.md).

The compact snapshot contains the complete published result tables and figures used here. Raw reads, reference/index files, complete Salmon estimates, fitted R objects and the full GO association snapshot are acquired or generated by the workflow and are not redistributed. The running guide distinguishes inspection of supplied outputs, small synthetic checks, environment restoration and a full new execution. A second complete reconstruction on an independently provisioned machine has not been run.

The accompanying offline checker verifies primary tables, selected-list consistency, enrichment summaries, comparison ratios, diagnostic-report resources and snapshot hashes. These arithmetic and file-integrity checks complement the recorded model execution; they do not provide independent biological replication.

## Reconstruction scope

The original study's exact trimming, index/import parameters and DESeq2 version are not recoverable from its methods. This reconstruction uses archived reads matching the clean-read totals and reports the replacement settings. KEGG, host-parasite networks and qPCR analyses are outside the executed RNA-seq reconstruction. No additional animal experiment or independent cohort was analyzed. Source attribution is retained in the main report and repository notice.

## Supporting diagnostics

The [detailed results](../docs/results.md) retain mapping coverage, nominal-P sensitivity counts, example gene effects, the overlapping-locus calculation and the published-list comparison. Sample-level diagnostics support the decision to retain all libraries. They describe observed sample behavior without assigning a technical cause, contamination status or parasite burden from expression patterns alone.

Numerical P values stored as zero in a few day-33 rows reflect floating-point underflow. Figures use a finite plotting floor. Saved-model diagnostics retain Cook flags and the pinned DESeq2 count-based exceptions; no outlier replacement or sample removal was performed. The [diagnostic HTML appendix](../example-results/report/index.html) provides additional plots in their original directory structure.
