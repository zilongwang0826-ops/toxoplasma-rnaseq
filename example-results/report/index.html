Mouse brain response to Toxoplasma gondii infection

Reanalysis of PRJNA483261; infected versus control at 11 and 33 days. Hu et al. (2020).

Interpretation limits

Whole-brain changes may reflect both cell composition and within-cell regulation. Unrecorded batch effects and pooling uncertainty cannot be resolved statistically. Enrichment is exploratory and does not establish pathway activation or causality. Published hit counts are reference observations, not targets used for optimization. GO is the primary functional analysis; inspect kegg-status.txt for KEGG execution status.
